i · Content and ranking signals

Engagement signals and follower conversion

The ranking system weights interactions unequally, and the material that accumulates followers differs from the material that accumulates likes. This section concerns both.

Content with instrumental value

Material that a reader can reuse, a prompt, a workflow, a technique, converts viewers into followers at a higher rate than material that is only entertaining, since the reader has reason to expect comparable output later. The post below publishes its prompt in full:

Media attachment and initial reach

Posts carrying an image or a video receive wider initial distribution than text alone. Where a photograph or clip can carry the content, including one is the higher-yield option.

Mechanism. Media posts receive additional feed placement before any engagement has accrued.

Replies as a distribution signal

Each reply extends a post's circulation. Posts phrased to require an answer therefore travel further than declarative statements, which give the reader no occasion to respond.

Mechanism. Replies outweigh reposts, bookmarks and likes in the ranking, and replies on a post from an account the reader follows now carry a fifteenfold weighting.
ii · Timing and search behavior

Entering existing attention flows

Attention concentrates around events and terminology already in circulation. Material that enters one of these flows inherits an audience assembled by other means, provided it arrives while the flow persists.

Lexical alignment with current discourse

Retrieval depends on wording. A post describing a phenomenon in terms other than those presently in circulation will not surface for the readers searching that phenomenon, however closely its content bears on the subject.

Mechanism. The ranking system matches post text against active queries, so wording determines whether a post is retrievable at all.

iii · Accounts without an audience

Growth in the absence of distribution

A new account receives minimal circulation, so material published to its own timeline reaches almost no one and attracts no engagement to compound. Under this constraint, participation in established threads is the more productive channel.

Reply placement on low-engagement posts

On a post carrying few responses, a single reply constitutes a substantial share of the visible conversation and is likely to be read and answered by the author. The post below stands at eleven likes:

iv · Access to established audiences

Transfer from established accounts

A repost from an account with a large following transfers a portion of that audience directly, and does so faster than any amount of unassisted publication. The methods below concern how such attention is obtained, including the adversarial case.

Documented collaboration over proximity

A photograph establishing proximity to a notable person conveys no information and gives their audience no reason to respond. A record of work performed together conveys both. The example below documents a modified conference badge and credits the collaborator:

Ivoine In SF@ivoinetech · Aug 8

I hacked my badge at defcon and turned it into a camera. @0dayCTF #defcon #defcon34

27 56 1.3K 82K

Mention as a distribution mechanism

A mention places the post in the notification stream of the account named. Where the content reflects favorably on that account, the mention also supplies an incentive to repost it to their own following, which is the outcome of consequence.

Mechanism. A repost from a large account is the most direct transfer of followers available on the platform.

Disagreement and adversarial circulation

A contested position invites quotation, and readers who oppose the quoting account frequently follow the account quoted. The platform has since reduced the circulation of purely inflammatory material, which confines the method to substantive disagreement:

Nikita Bier@nikitabier · Aug 18

The largest contributor of seeing ragebait was the reply predictor and we were aware that angry replies were causing people to see more of that content. So last month, we gave the reply predictor a 15x boost if it’s a friend’s post—and it reduced ragebait by order of magnitude.

v · Offline gatherings

Conversion from physical gatherings

Attendance supplies two things at once: original material unavailable to those not present, and direct contact with potential followers. Neither is commonly exploited by attendees.

Documentation of live events

Photographing the most unusual object present and naming the event combines original material with an active search term, compounding two of the effects described above on a single post. The example below was taken and published on the floor:

Max Med@MozeTech · Aug 9

met the thanos of defcon, dude hooked up a flipper zero to this glove so now he can flick his fingers and hack your shit

4 43 2K

Direction of the follow request

Asking a new contact to follow the account, rather than offering to follow theirs, transfers the action to them while the meeting is still in progress. Where the initiative is left with the other party, the connection is frequently never completed. Stating that the application is not installed on the phone is sufficient to redirect the request.

Mechanism. The party who performs the action at the time is the party who has committed to it.
vi · Publication of ongoing work

Continuous publication of work

Most work of any interest is never published. Where no confidentiality constraint applies, output that already exists can be converted into material at negligible additional cost, which is the cheapest source of supply available.

Small artifacts aligned with the account's subject

A minor application related to the account's stated domain supplies readers with grounds to expect further work of the same kind, and production cost is low under current tooling. The example below documents a combat prototype:
