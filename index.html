Attach a picture or a video

Plain text tweets are fine, but the algorithm simply pushes media harder. If a photo or video can carry the post, it should.

Why it works — media posts earn more feed placement before anyone has engaged with them.

Replies push your post into feeds

Every reply on your post extends its reach. Posts that invite an answer, a question, an offer to share the prompt, outperform polished statements nobody feels like responding to.

Why it works — reply beats retweet beats bookmark beats like, and X now weights replies on a friend's post 15x. Write posts your circle has to answer.

Use the words in the zeitgeist

Whatever is going viral this week, your post needs those exact words. If "AI escaping its sandbox" is the story and you're at a demo of something like it, say it in those terms. This matters more than almost anything else.

Why it works — the algorithm matches your words against what people search. Wrong words, invisible post.

iii · Starting from zero

Small accounts grow in other people's threads

The algorithm is not kind to new accounts. Your post reaches 40 people and nobody interacts, because nobody knows you. So don't post. Reply.

iv · Borrowing audiences

Borrow a bigger audience

One retweet from the right person outperforms months of posting into the void. It can be earned, and there's a more combative route too.

A selfie is not a story

"Met this guy today" does nothing. Do something worth posting and tag who it involves. A hacked badge beats any handshake photo, 82K views:

Ivoine In SF@ivoinetech · Aug 8

I hacked my badge at defcon and turned it into a camera. @0dayCTF #defcon #defcon34

27 56 1.3K 82K

Tag them, every time

Two things happen when you tag the person in a post about something genuinely good you did with them: the post reaches their audience, and they feel compelled to retweet it, because it reflects well on them too.

Why it works — a retweet from a large account is the fastest follower transfer on the platform.

Disagreement is a strategy

Contrarian takes get you quoted, and the quote-tweeter's haters become your followers. One caveat straight from X's head of product: pure ragebait just got nerfed, so disagree with substance:

Nikita Bier@nikitabier · Aug 18

The largest contributor of seeing ragebait was the reply predictor and we were aware that angry replies were causing people to see more of that content. So last month, we gave the reply predictor a 15x boost if it’s a friend’s post—and it reduced ragebait by order of magnitude.

v · In person

Rooms convert better than feeds

People are at events every week, especially in SF, and almost nobody turns those rooms into followers.

Shoot what's genuinely interesting

Photograph the coolest thing in the room, name the event, and post while you're still standing there. Event name, keyword, and a photo stack three boosts on one post:

Have them add you, not the reverse

Ask everyone you meet whether they're on X, then have them follow you on the spot. The direction matters: if you add them, they take you as a follower and forget you. A simple excuse covers it: you don't have the app on your phone, so they should add you.

Why it works — people flake. The person who taps follow is the one who committed.
